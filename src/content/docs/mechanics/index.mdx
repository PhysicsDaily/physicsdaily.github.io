**Mechanics** is the branch of physics that asks two questions about every moving body: *how does it move?* and *why does it move that way?* From a rolling ball to an orbiting planet, the same handful of principles describe them all.

<Callout type="definition" title="Two Halves of Mechanics">
**Kinematics** describes motion — position, velocity, and acceleration — without asking what causes it. **Dynamics** explains the causes, through forces, energy, and the laws that link them to motion.
</Callout>

## The Path Through Mechanics

We build mechanics in layers. First comes the mathematical language of **vectors**, since position, velocity, and force all carry a direction. With that language in hand, we describe motion itself in kinematics, and later explain it with forces and energy. Each chapter leans on the one before it, so it is best read in order.
